Netflix

Migrating our distributed cache to a multi-region architecture

✦ AI Summary

Netflix rewrote their caching layer to survive regional failures — here's the tradeoffs they accepted.

  • Moved from single-region Memcached to multi-region active-active, cutting cache miss latency by 40%
  • Eliminated cross-region fallback reads that caused cascading slowdowns during outages
  • Accepted eventual consistency for non-critical data — a deliberate tradeoff over strong consistency

Airbnb

Building a real-time feature store for ML models at scale

✦ AI Summary

Airbnb open-sourced their feature store Chronon — built to fix the training-serving skew problem that plagues most ML teams.

  • Training-serving skew — where features computed at training time differ from serving time — was causing silent model degradation
  • Chronon unifies feature definitions so the same logic runs in batch, streaming, and real-time serving
  • Reduced feature engineering time by ~50% across Airbnb's ML teams and cut data leakage incidents significantly
